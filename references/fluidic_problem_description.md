# Comprehensive Problem Description: Coupled Thermal-Biochemical Microfluidic System

This document describes a mathematical model of a **2D microfluidic growth chip coupled with an external 1D substrate supply tube**, incorporating **substrate mass transport, bacterial growth kinetics, fluid flow, and microbial heat generation with full temperature evolution**.

```text
       y = Ly  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │  Closed Boundary (no-flux: ∇C·n = 0, ∇X·n = 0, -k∇T·n = q_loss)  │
               │                                                                 │
               │   2D Microfluidic Chip Domain (Ω_chip)                          │
               │   • Substrate Diffusion & Reaction: ∂C/∂t = D_C ∇²C - R_C        │
               │   • Biomass Growth & Decay:       ∂X/∂t = D_X ∇²X + μ(C)X - δX  │
               │   • Heat Transport & Generation:  ρ c_p ∂T/∂t = k ∇²T + Q_bio   │
               │                                                                 │
   y = 0 (Γ)   ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤  <-- 1D–2D Interface
               ========== 1D Supply Tube Domain (Ω_tube) =========================>     (Flux & Thermal
               Uniform Flow Velocity (u₀), Substrate Input (C_in), Inlet Temp (T_in)    Continuity)
```

---

## 1. System Domains & Geometry

1. **2D Microfluidic Chip Domain ($\Omega_{\text{chip}}$):**\
   A rectangular growth chamber defined over spatial coordinates $(x, y) \in [0, L_x] \times [0, L_y]$. The chip contains stagnant or low-velocity fluid, where substrate transport occurs primarily via diffusion, and bacterial biomass expands locally.

2. **1D External Supply Tube Domain ($\Omega_{\text{tube}}$):**\
   A narrow, high-aspect-ratio supply channel lying parallel along the open boundary at $y = 0$, defined along $x \in [0, L_x]$. A controlled, uniform fluid velocity $u_0$ delivers substrate and regulates thermal conditions at the inlet ($x = 0$).

3. **Coupling Interface ($\Gamma_{\text{int}}$ at $y = 0$):**\
   An open boundary connecting the 1D supply line and the 2D chip chamber, allowing continuous lateral exchange of substrate mass and thermal energy via diffusion and conduction.

---

## 2. Governing Coupled Equations

### A. Substrate Transport ($C$)
* **In the 2D Chip ($\Omega_{\text{chip}}$):** Substrate diffuses and is consumed by metabolic activity:
  $$\frac{\partial C_{\text{chip}}}{\partial t} = D_C \nabla^2 C_{\text{chip}} - \underbrace{\frac{\mu(C_{\text{chip}})}{Y_{X/C}} X}_{\text{Consumption Rate } R_C}$$

* **Along the 1D Supply Tube ($\Omega_{\text{tube}}$):** Substrate is advected downstream while losing mass into the chip across the interface:
  $$\frac{\partial C_{\text{tube}}}{\partial t} + u_0 \frac{\partial C_{\text{tube}}}{\partial x} = D_C \frac{\partial^2 C_{\text{tube}}}{\partial x^2} - \frac{J_C(x, t)}{H_{\text{tube}}}$$
  where $J_C(x, t) = -D_C \left. \frac{\partial C_{\text{chip}}}{\partial y} \right|_{y=0}$ is the diffusive substrate flux into the chip.

---

### B. Bacterial Biomass Dynamics ($X$)
Bacterial density expands in the 2D chip via Monod-type substrate consumption, undergoes random motility/spatial expansion, and decays at a rate $\delta$:
$$\frac{\partial X}{\partial t} = D_X \nabla^2 X + \mu(C_{\text{chip}}, T_{\text{chip}}) X - \delta X$$

where the temperature-dependent growth rate $\mu(C, T)$ combines **Monod kinetics** with a thermal scaling function (e.g., Cardinal Temperature Model / Ratkowsky equation):
$$\mu(C, T) = \mu_{\max}(T) \cdot \frac{C}{K_s + C}$$

---

### C. Heat Transport & Microbial Heat Release ($T$)

Microbial respiration and metabolic carbon conversion are exothermic processes. The volumetric heat generation rate $Q_{\bio}$ is proportional to the substrate consumption rate:

$$Q_{\bio}(x, y, t) = Y_{Q/C} \cdot R_C(C, X) = Y_{Q/C} \cdot \frac{\mu(C, T)}{Y_{X/C}} X$$

where $Y_{Q/C}$ is the metabolic enthalpy/heat yield per unit mass of substrate consumed ($\text{J/g}$).

* **Thermal Evolution in the 2D Chip ($\Omega_{\text{chip}}$):**
  $$\rho_{\text{chip}} c_{p, \text{chip}} \frac{\partial T_{\text{chip}}}{\partial t} = k_{\text{chip}} \nabla^2 T_{\text{chip}} + Q_{\bio}(x, y, t)$$
  *(Heat spreads via conduction through the fluid/biomass matrix and is generated by local bacterial metabolism).*

* **Thermal Evolution along the 1D Supply Tube ($\Omega_{\text{tube}}$):**
  $$\rho_{\text{tube}} c_{p, \text{tube}} \left( \frac{\partial T_{\text{tube}}}{\partial t} + u_0 \frac{\partial T_{\text{tube}}}{\partial x} \right) = k_{\text{tube}} \frac{\partial^2 T_{\text{tube}}}{\partial x^2} - \frac{J_Q(x, t)}{H_{\text{tube}}}$$
  where $J_Q(x, t) = -k_{\text{chip}} \left. \frac{\partial T_{\text{chip}}}{\partial y} \right|_{y=0}$ is the conductive heat flux exchanged between the chip and the tube across the interface.

---

## 3. Boundary Conditions & Inter-Domain Coupling

1. **Substrate & Thermal Conditions at Tube Inlet ($x = 0$):**
   $$C_{\text{tube}}(0, t) = C_{\in}, \quad T_{\text{tube}}(0, t) = T_{\in}$$

2. **Substrate & Thermal Outflow at Tube Outlet ($x = L_x$):**
   $$\frac{\partial C_{\text{tube}}}{\partial x} = 0, \quad \frac{\partial T_{\text{tube}}}{\partial x} = 0$$

3. **Interface Coupling at $y = 0$ ($x \in [0, L_x]$):**
   * **Dirichlet Continuity:**\
     $$C_{\text{chip}}(x, 0, t) = C_{\text{tube}}(x, t), \quad T_{\text{chip}}(x, 0, t) = T_{\text{tube}}(x, t)$$
   * **Neumann Flux Balance:**\
     The diffusive substrate flux $J_C(x,t)$ and conductive heat flux $J_Q(x,t)$ leaving the 1D line serve directly as the entry flux into the 2D chip boundary.

4. **Closed Outer Chip Walls ($x=0, x=L_x, y=L_y$):**
   * **Substrate & Bacteria:** No-flux condition: $\mathbf{n} \cdot \nabla C = 0$, $\mathbf{n} \cdot \nabla X = 0$.
   * **Temperature:** Either insulated (adiabatic, $\mathbf{n} \cdot \nabla T = 0$) or convective ambient heat loss across the chip walls:
     $$-k_{\text{chip}} (\mathbf{n} \cdot \nabla T_{\text{chip}}) = h_{\text{amb}} (T_{\text{chip}} - T_{\text{ambient}})$$

---

## 4. Key Physical Regimes & Coupling Loops

* **Bi-directional Thermal-Biochemical Feedback:**
  $$\text{Substrate Supply } (C) \longrightarrow \text{Metabolic Growth } (\mu) \longrightarrow \text{Heat Generation } (Q_{\bio}) \longrightarrow \text{Temp Rise } (T) \longrightarrow \text{Altered Growth Rate } (\mu(T))$$
* **Péclet Number for Heat vs. Mass ($\text{Pe}_T$ vs. $\text{Pe}_C$):**\
  Because thermal diffusivity ($\alpha = \frac{k}{\rho c_p} \sim 10^{-7} \text{ m}^2/\text{s}$) is typically 100 to 1000 times larger than substrate diffusivity ($D_C \sim 10^{-9} \text{ m}^2/\text{s}$), heat flattens across the chip domain much faster than substrate concentration.
